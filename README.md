# Mykola Mykhailian

**AI-native product designer and engineer** · Palo Alto, CA

I design products and then build them, from research and interaction design through design systems to code that ships. My work runs on an AI-native workflow: I prototype in code from day one, use LLMs as collaborators throughout the process, and put AI features into products where they actually help people.

### What I'm building

- **Odvi** – Consumer finance apps, starting with *Odvi Payoff*, a debt payoff planner for iOS. It's a monorepo with a shared money-math engine (fully tested) and its own design-token system covering color, type, spacing and motion, in light and dark.
- **App Portfolio** – A family of original iOS apps on shared infrastructure (storage, purchases, analytics, AI, auth). Each app keeps its own design and voice. The first wave ships in December 2026.
- **Assay** – A curated marketplace for antique and vintage fine jewelry.

*These are in private development; case studies are on [mykola.design](https://mykola.design).*
